**1. Introductions: what do we actually do?**

- Introduce yourself, your role, and the developer community you work with.
- How do you explain your job to someone outside tech?
- What’s one thing people assume you do that’s completely wrong?

**2. What is Developer Relations?**

- How would each of us define DevRel?
- Who are we helping, and what problems are we trying to solve?
- How does DevRel overlap with engineering, marketing, product, and community?
- Why can the same job title mean very different things at different companies?

**3. How we ended up here**

- What were we doing before DevRel?
- Was this a career we deliberately pursued, or something we stumbled into?
- What helped us land that first opportunity?
- Which skills from our previous roles turned out to be surprisingly useful?
- What do we wish we’d known before starting?

*Natural transition: “So that’s how we got here—what does the job actually look like now?”*

**4. The reality behind the job**

- What does a typical week look like? Does a typical week even exist?
- How much time goes into coding, writing, speaking, community work, and internal meetings?
- What work happens behind the scenes that people rarely see?
- What’s the best part—and what’s harder than it looks?
- Any memorable demo disasters or lessons learned the hard way?

**5. The questions people might hesitate to ask**

- Do you need to be a strong programmer to work in DevRel?
- Can you succeed if you’re introverted or dislike public speaking?
- Do you need a big social media following?
- Is DevRel part of sales? What happens when helping a developer doesn’t lead to a purchase?
- How honest can you be about your own product’s limitations?
- How do you handle a developer’s feedback when your company doesn’t want to hear it?
- How do you prove your work is making a difference?

**6. Building a career without burning out**

- What would we suggest someone try before applying for a DevRel role?
- What makes a useful portfolio? Does it need talks, code, or content?
- What questions should candidates ask to understand what a company expects from DevRel?
- How do we set boundaries around travel, events, and being available online?
- How do we keep enjoying our hobbies when they overlap with our jobs?
- Where can a DevRel career lead?

**7. Audience questions and closing thoughts**

- Take audience questions throughout, with extra time here for anything unanswered.
- What’s one belief about DevRel we’ve changed our minds about?
- What’s one practical next step for someone curious about the field?
- Finish with one piece of advice from each speaker.
